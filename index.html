What We Do

OUR SERVICES

🔷 01
Moulds

Design and manufacture of precision moulds for plastic injection, compression, blow moulding and more — built to exact tolerances for high-volume production runs.

⚙️ 02
Dies

Progressive, compound and blanking dies crafted for sheet metal forming operations. Designed for repeatability, durability, and consistent performance on the shop floor.

🔩 03
Jigs

Custom drilling, welding and assembly jigs engineered to guide tools and workpieces with precision — eliminating errors and speeding up production cycles.

📐 04
Fixtures

Robust machining and inspection fixtures that securely locate and hold components during manufacturing — ensuring dimensional accuracy and part-to-part consistency.

⚡ 05
Spark EDM

Sparkonix EDM sinker operations for intricate cavities, deep holes, and complex geometries that conventional machining cannot achieve.

🪢 06
Wire EDM

High-precision CNC wire-cut EDM job work for punch-die sets, intricate profiles, and exotic materials — with tight tolerances on every pass.
